About us

Educators first. Consultants second.

Here to Learn Consultants is a network of experienced educational consultants supporting schools across the Asia region.

Our story

Between us we have many years of experience, both in the classroom and as school leaders. We have started schools, written curricula, coached leaders and guided schools through improvement and growth.

We came together as a network so that schools could access the right expertise for each challenge, without the overhead of a large firm. Our consultants are skilled in school start-up, policy and curriculum development, leadership coaching and people management, school improvement and strategic growth.

We offer online and in-person advice, coaching, consultancy and training, and we are proud of the name we chose: whatever a school’s stage, we are here to learn with it.

What we value

Principles that guide our work.

01

Learning comes first

Every recommendation we make is tested against one question: will it improve learning for students?

02

Context matters

No two schools are the same. We tailor our support to your culture, community and stage of development.

03

Honest partnership

We say what we see, with care. Trust and candour are what make external support worth having.

04

Build capacity

We measure success by how strong your own team is when our work is done.
