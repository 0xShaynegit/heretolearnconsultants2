Services

Expert support for every stage of your school.

Four areas of expertise, delivered as advice, coaching, consultancy or training, online or in person across Asia.

01School start-up

Opening a school, done right from day one.

Launching a school is a complex mix of education, regulation, finance and people. We have opened schools ourselves and can guide founders, investors and boards through each decision.

Feasibility & visionMarket context, educational model and a clear founding vision.
Licensing & complianceNavigating local requirements and preparing documentation.
Leadership & recruitmentStructures, job profiles and hiring your founding team.
Launch readinessTimetables, systems, policies and opening day planning.
02Policy & curriculum

A curriculum and policy framework that works in practice.

We help schools design, review and implement curricula and policies that reflect their mission, satisfy inspection and accreditation, and make sense to the teachers who use them.

Curriculum design & reviewCoherent, well-sequenced programmes from early years upward.
Policy developmentClear, compliant policies across safeguarding, teaching, HR and more.
AssessmentMeaningful assessment and reporting that informs teaching.
Accreditation preparationReadiness reviews and support through the accreditation journey.
03Leadership & people

Stronger leaders, stronger teams.

Great schools are led well. We coach principals, heads of school and middle leaders, and help leadership teams recruit, develop and retain the people who make the difference.

Executive coachingConfidential one-to-one coaching for senior leaders.
Middle leadershipProgrammes that build the next generation of school leaders.
People managementPerformance, appraisal, wellbeing and difficult conversations.
Team developmentBuilding aligned, effective senior leadership teams.
04Improvement & growth

Clear priorities, sustainable growth.

We offer honest external perspective and practical strategy, helping schools raise standards, strengthen their reputation and grow in a way that lasts.

School reviewAn independent, evidence-based view of strengths and next steps.
Strategic planningMulti-year plans your board, staff and community can own.
Quality assuranceSystems for monitoring teaching, learning and outcomes.
Growth & enrolmentPositioning, expansion planning and community engagement.
